4 Quick Steps When Recording Your Video (Text to 848-204-0790 or email ken@apextransitionsnj.com):

  • Move Slowly & Pause 2 Seconds on Each Side: Walk slowly around each piece of furniture and hold the camera still for 2 full seconds on every side (front, left, right, back, and top) so we can verify its exact condition without needing a long in-person inspection.
  • Open Drawers If You Can: Gently open dresser, desk, and cabinet drawers on camera so we can check inside cleanly.
  • Pan Steadily Across Smalls: Record a slow, steady sweep over boxed or tabletop household items (kitchenware, decor, tools, collectibles).
  • Share Honest Condition Notes Upfront: Please let us know right away if items come from a smoking home (even if smoked around 20 years ago) or have pet/urine odors, as odor-affected items will be rejected upon arrival. We stay away from upholstered sofas and mattresses unless they are pristine, stain-free, odor-free, and like-new.

OUR JOURNEY: 10 YEARS OF LIFTING UP NEIGHBORS & FAMILIES

For the past 10 years, while working full time, I raised my family as a local garage-sale flipper and word-of-mouth furniture and household-goods rehomer—feeding my family by helping neighbors lighten their loads without ever running a single advertisement. Along the way, my own family and close friends stood right beside me—helping clean out these houses, pack up rooms, and carry heavy pieces together. Over that decade, working at a steady, honest pace earned us a loyal circle of happy homeowners, realtors, and senior-community front desks who knew Ken as their trusted go-to call.

Now that my kids are grown, there are fewer hands around our own dinner table to carry the other end of a dresser or pack up a house full of small treasures. That led us to open Apex Transitions LLC—allowing us to bring that same humble, neighborly spirit full-time into a true top-shelf, fully insured service that lifts up others along the way.

Because it would be impossible for me to rehome every household on our own, we partnered with vetted local families and community entrepreneurs who sell your usable items and keep more than the lion's share of every dollar—putting real income onto local tables and teaching great work ethic to their kids, while the remainder goes directly toward lowering your upfront cleanout bill.
